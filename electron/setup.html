Heddohon

The address of your Heddohon server, as you open it in a browser.

A sign-in in front of the server

A proxy that asks you to sign in first (Authelia, Authentik, Cloudflare Access) shows its page in this window, and one that asks for a name and password is answered from a prompt. For a proxy that takes a header instead, such as a service token, add it here, one to a line. It is sent to the server's address and to no other.

The app is a window onto that server: your music, your account and your settings stay on it. Change the address later from the menu (press Alt), under Heddohon.
